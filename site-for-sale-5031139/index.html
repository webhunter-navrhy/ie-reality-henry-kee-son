Location
Gortnagrace, Castlefin

A one acre site located in the townland of Gortnagrace. Presently laid out as a rectangular shaped, good quality grassland field which has extensive road frontage onto a peaceful, minor public road. Gently sloping with a west-facing aspect, the plot offers commanding rural views over the Finn Valley. Located almost midway between the towns of Castlefin (5km) and Castlederg (4km). Mains electricity and mains water are all beside this site. All enquiries welcome.
